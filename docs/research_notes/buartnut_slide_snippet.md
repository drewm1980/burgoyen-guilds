# Buartnut slide snippet (ready to paste)

Files created by the photo fork; the deck files were not edited. Paste the slides below into `docs/garden_guilds.marp.md` and `docs/garden_guilds.nl.marp.md` (after the 'Heartnut needs a partner' slide), then run `./build.sh`. Buartnut is the owner's second choice after two heartnuts, and ahead of a single 'Broadview' walnut.

## 1. Suggested wording for the existing 'Heartnut needs a partner' slide (preference order)

English:

```
# Heartnut needs a partner

- A lone heartnut rarely sets nuts.
- Best: a second heartnut, planted close (~5–6 m).
- Second choice: a buartnut. Third: one 'Broadview' walnut.
```

Dutch:

```
# Hartnoot heeft een partner nodig

- Eén enkele hartnoot zet zelden noten.
- Beste: een tweede hartnoot, dicht erbij (~5–6 m).
- Tweede keus: een buartnoot. Derde: één 'Broadview'-walnoot.
```

Keep the existing buy lines and speaker note; add to the speaker note: "Preference order from the owner: 1 second heartnut, 2 buartnut, 3 single Broadview walnut."

## 5. Caveats

- `buartnut_fruit_cluster.jpg` (about 270 px wide) and `buartnut_shells_pile.jpg` (about 290 px) are low resolution: fine at thumbnail size, soft when enlarged as a right-hand background.
- Nekrasov scans have a small Cyrillic signature and show the kernel still in its shell, not cracked-out halves.
- The snippet says 'Mitchell' in the title text because that is what De Nootsaeck sells; only the tree photo is labelled Mitchell by its vendor.
