## Not found
- **C. mollissima nuts or burs (Grimo/Hebei type):** Commons has only flowering/catkin photos (Zhangzhugang, Hangzhou Botanical Garden, CC BY-SA 4.0) and old book scans. Eetbaargoed pages show only saplings (see chestnut_verify.md). The existing 'Qing' nuts and Canopy Nursery bur photos are still the best available.
- **Heartnut nuts / buartnut nuts:** Commons has a buartnut tree (leaves only, discarded) and small Nekrasov photos (432×624 and 572×438, CC BY-SA 3.0: "Juglans cinerea hybrid Juglans ailantifolia var. cordiformis") that were not downloaded. Food Forest Nursery and Stark Bro's returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool. Try Eetbaargoed's other heartnut cultivar pages or a manual browser save.
- **Ripe damson/kroosjes, round and small:** candidates on Commons not yet viewed: "Kriecherl 3928.jpg" (CC BY 3.0 at), "Damsons (Prunus domestica subsp. insititia) (19884486631).jpg" (CC BY-SA 2.0).
- **Existing images over 500 KB (not touched by this pass):** `judas_tree_nancy.png` (2.0 MB), `sativa_nuts.jpg` (2.3 MB), `sativa_bur.jpg` (2.0 MB). Worth shrinking for the web page.
